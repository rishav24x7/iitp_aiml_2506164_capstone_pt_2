# Manual Review — The Calls That Aren't Obvious (Part 2)

Segments are useful, but some customers don't fit a tidy rule — usually because high value collides with a warning sign like returns or angry tickets. Below are twelve real customers where I'd want a human to make the call, with what I'd actually do and why. All numbers are as of the 2025-09-30 snapshot. I've noted the real churn outcome at the end of each, but only as a sanity check — it played no part in the decision.
